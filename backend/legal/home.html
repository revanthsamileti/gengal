GenGal

Connect & vibe

GenGal is a social calling app for adults in India. Meet new people, chat, and talk on voice and video calls.

How it works

  • Sign in with your Indian mobile number. You verify it by sending us one SMS or WhatsApp message with a code the app shows you.
  • Browse people who are online, follow them, and chat.
  • Voice and video calls are paid for with coins bought in the app. We do not record calls.
  • GenGal is for people aged 18 and over only.

Safety

You can block or report anyone from their profile. Accounts that break our terms may be suspended or removed.
